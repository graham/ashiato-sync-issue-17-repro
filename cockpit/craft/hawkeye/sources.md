# E-2D Advanced Hawkeye visual reference

The runtime exterior is an original procedural model made from Godot primitives. It
contains no downloaded mesh, photograph, texture, trademark, or squadron livery.

NAVAIR's official [E-2D product page](https://www.navair.navy.mil/product/E-2D)
identifies the carrier airborne-early-warning role, five-person crew, twin turboprops,
and rotodome configuration. The checked native/model contract uses the established
17.60 m length, 24.56 m span, and 7.32 m rotodome diameter recorded in the model's test
sheet. The twin nacelles, four-blade propellers, high tail, rotodome, folding wing panels,
side-by-side pilots, and port-facing mission operators are its defining cues.

Datum and axes: the craft origin is the centre of the fuselage envelope, forward is
`-Z`, up is `+Y`, and one Godot unit is one metre. The fold command is authoritative
shared state. Native collision, mass/inertia, carrier contact, networking, and all four
station poses remain authoritative.
